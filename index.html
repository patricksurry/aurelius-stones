Meditations

Marcus Aurelius · AD 121–180

Τὰ εἰς ἑαυτόν · to himself

Marcus Aurelius wrote these notes as a spiritual exercise, returning to the same moral principles again and again.

Here they are gathered into 48 touchstones, to contemplate by theme or read straight through.

Reflect in your own words to claim each stone as yours.

Translated by A. S. L. Farquharson, 1944.
