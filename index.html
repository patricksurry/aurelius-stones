Meditations

Τὰ εἰς ἑαυτόν · to himself

Marcus Aurelius wrote these notes to himself. He came back to the same few ideas again and again.

Here they are gathered into 48 touchstones.

Read slowly. Put a stone in your own words to make it yours.
