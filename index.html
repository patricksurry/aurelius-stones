Meditations

Τὰ εἰς ἑαυτόν · to himself

Marcus Aurelius, emperor of Rome, kept a notebook of reminders to himself. He never meant anyone else to read it.

Its 488 passages rest here on 48 stones: the thoughts he kept returning to, about how to see, what to accept, and how to act.

Read a passage slowly. Move through its stone. When something lands, put it in your own words, and the stone is yours.
